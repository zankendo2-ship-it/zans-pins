## 1. Background research (written before the app check)

What we can and can't know: TikTok only shows the exact video behind each strike inside the app (Settings > Support > Safety Center > Account status). From outside we can only match its rules against what the account posted.

TikTok's rules (sources: TikTok ads policy "Gambling and Games", Community Guidelines on regulated goods, trade press 4 to 6 Nov 2026):
- TikTok does not allow content that markets gambling. It reaffirmed this ban on 4 to 6 Nov 2026, the same week as the first strike.
- "Gambling information" (insights, stats or strategies meant to improve betting outcomes) is a restricted category.
- Strikes expire after 90 days. Deleting a video does not remove its strike. A successful appeal does.
- Expiry dates: strike of 6 Nov ends about 4 Feb 2027; strike of 13 Nov ends about 11 Feb 2027.

What the account posted to TikTok around those dates (from Metricool):
| Date | Post | Why it is risky |
|---|---|---|
| 30 Oct | Breeders' Cup guide posts with "18+ Have fun and always gamble responsibly" (21,830 and 5,563 views) | Labels itself as gambling content; sells a betting guide |
| 31 Oct | Three Breeders' Cup "tonight" posts with race times | Race-day content pushing a guide |

Most likely causes: the 3 Nov selections post and the Breeders' Cup guide posts for the first strike; the Cheltenham race-day posts of 12 to 13 Nov for the second. Unconfirmed until Alex checks the app.

How we avoid a third (already in the TikTok Strike Shield, plus new points marked NEW):
- Gift and merch posts only. No race-day content, previews, race times, stats, selections or guides.
- No "18+" or "gamble responsibly" wording on any TikTok post (it tells TikTok the post is about gambling).
- NEW: showcase baubles whose hook is about a real horse's results or news stay off TikTok. TikTok gets pure gift hooks only.
- NEW (Alex to decide): remove "18+ Gamble responsibly" from the TikTok bio, and delete or hide the old race-day and selections videos so they can't be flagged again.
