# Why views are low, and what to change (19 Nov 2026)

Source: Metricool, every TikTok video, Instagram Reel, Facebook Reel and YouTube Short from 5 to 19 Nov 2026 (pulled 19 Nov 08:25 UTC). Internal only: never quote these figures in a post.
Not available here: the viral study and playbook in /mnt/project-files (not mounted in this session), views_log.csv, average watch time and 3-second hold (not in the fields pulled). Samples are small, so these are patterns, not proof.

## Findings
1. **TikTok has two levels and nothing in between.** 23 posts: 13 got 590 to 800 views, 10 got 180 to 270. Nothing got past 800, so no post has been pushed beyond TikTok's first test audience.
2. **What sits in the low group: anything that is a still picture.** All 4 photo posts (232 to 268), all 4 bauble videos (219, 244, 222, 237: one still photo with a slow zoom), the Old Park Star slides video (183) and the 14 Nov picks slide video (271). What sits in the high group: videos where the picture really moves or changes every second or two (mug designer and mug videos 647 to 798, race-illustration videos 664 to 796). TikTok appears to treat a slow zoom on one photo as a photo.
3. **The new locked bauble format is in the low group.** 18 Nov: three posts, 183, 222, 237. The face-off videos made last night use the same build (one still + slow zoom + a static card), so they are likely to land at about 200 too unless the picture moves.
4. **Hardly anyone reacts.** Best TikTok post: 41 likes and 3 comments on 798 views (the "9 handle colours" mug, which asked an easy choice question). Most posts: 0 to 5 likes, 0 comments, 0 shares. Over the whole fortnight on TikTok: 3 shares.
5. **Instagram Reels are not being shown at all: 0 to 31 views on 28 of 29 reels (best 107).** That is far below what even a tiny account gets, and it does not move with the content. Likely causes, in order: (a) the account is not being recommended (Account Status), (b) betting wording in recent captions ("18+ Only. Begambleaware.org", each-way Yankee, prices), (c) up to 6 reels a day (14 Nov), (d) web links and long credits in captions.
6. **Facebook Reels sit at about 200 whatever we post** (151 to 259, with 0 to 2 likes), and **YouTube Shorts mostly under 100** (best 254, "Cheltenham today"). Both are baseline reach with no pick-up.
7. **Volume has not helped.** 14 Nov had 6 Instagram reels and 7 YouTube Shorts; none did better than a normal day.

## Actions proposed (each needs Alex's yes)
1. **Make every video really move in the first second.** Minimum for product posts: 3 to 5 different shots in the first 6 seconds (wide scene, close on the name, the second product, the pair), cut every 1.2 to 1.5 seconds, with the hook words on screen from 0.5 s. No more single-photo slow zooms. I can build this today from the pictures we already have (crops and angles of the same scene, plus the 3D mug turning, which is real movement).
2. **Put the hook on screen from the start**, big, in the top third, for the first 2 seconds, and say it in the first breath. At present the first 4 to 11 seconds are a picture with no words.
3. **Shorter.** Target 9 to 14 seconds. Today's face-off cuts run 19 to 30 s; cut the Santa ending to a 2-second card (or drop the spoken Santa line) so the question lands before people leave.
4. **Double down on what earned comments:** easy choice questions with the choices on screen ("Which handle colour?" got 41 likes and 3 comments). Face-offs fit this: both names on screen from second one and "A or B?" as the question.
5. **Drop from TikTok:** photo posts and slide-card videos. Keep track-data posts off TikTok anyway (strike rules).
6. **Instagram: Alex checks Account Status first** (Profile, menu, Account Status: "can be recommended" or not). Until that is clear, more reels will not help. From today: one reel a day, no betting wording, no web links in the caption, 3 to 5 hashtags, alt text on.
7. **One post a day per network**, two on big race days, at the booked times (Metricool best times already used: TikTok and Instagram 18:00, YouTube 16:00, Facebook 12:00 or 17:00).
8. **Sounds:** our accounts are business accounts, so only sounds cleared for commercial use are safe (TikTok's Commercial Music Library, or our own). Proposal: keep our own voice and music in the file, and when booking TikTok ask Alex to add a trending commercial-library sound at low volume in the app, since Metricool cannot attach TikTok sounds.
9. **Hashtags:** keep 3 to 5; lead with what people search (#horseracing, #cheltenham or the race name off TikTok, #christmasgifts, #personalisedgifts). No evidence yet that any tag set changes views; treat as a test.
10. **Reply to every comment within the hour** and pin a question as the first comment; posts with comments are the only ones that showed any lift.
11. **Measure it:** tag each new post "moving cuts" or "still zoom" in the weekly log, and compare after 5 posts each.
