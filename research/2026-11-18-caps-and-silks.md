## 1. Grey Dawning (Dan Skelton)

**Body: "Red, black armlets". Confirmed, 3+ sources.**
- Already held: At The Races profile, Sky Sports 2025 Betfair Chase card (see the clashes file).
- New: Weatherbys "Owners' Registered Colours" lists (the official register, published in The Racing Calendar), entry "Kirkland, Mr Robert | Red, black armlets", in both the 1 July 2017 and 1 July 2018 lists. These are old lists, but the wording is unchanged on 2026 cards.
  - https://support.racingadmin.co.uk/opencms/export/sites/weatherbys/racing-admin-support/.galleries/racing-admin-downloads/Colours-registered-as-of-1st-July-2017.pdf
  - https://support.racingadmin.co.uk/opencms/export/sites/weatherbys/Downloads/-colsupp-2018.pdf
- New: At The Races card for the 2026 Cheltenham Gold Cup (13 Mar 2026): "Red, black armlets (Mr Robert Kirkland)". https://www.attheraces.com/racecard/Cheltenham/13-March-2026/1600

**Cap: no source states one.** Not on the register entries, the 2025 Betfair Chase card or the 2026 Gold Cup card. No race report or photo caption found that describes the cap.

**What an unstated cap means (1 authoritative source, read as a search extract only):**
- BHA "Registration of Owners' Colours Guidelines": where "the cap is the same as the body it is not necessary to repeat it". The same document says colours already given need not be repeated, with the example "BLACK, WHITE sleeves, hooped cap" being enough to describe a black and white hooped cap. https://media.britishhorseracing.com/bha/rules/Ownership/Owners_Colours_Guidelines.pdf
- Supporting, general: colours are stated in the order jacket, sleeves, cap. https://www.hambletonracing.co.uk/racehorse-names-and-racing-colours/
- **So by the BHA wording rule, "Red, black armlets" means a plain red cap.** This is the rule applied to the description. It is NOT a sighting of the cap. The exact BHA sentence came through as a fragment, so read the PDF or check one race photo before drawing. Status: **red cap, 1 source, unconfirmed by eye.**
- Do not use "black cap, red star": that belongs to a different owner's colours (noted in the clashes file).

- **Irish cards: "Emerald green & orange hooped, white cap".** 2 sources:
  - Horse Racing Ireland racecards (many, same owner's runners). Example: https://www.hri.ie/racecards/details?meeting=2026-341&race=1604
  - Sky Sports card and result for the 2025 John Durkan (Fact To File): "EMERALD GREEN & ORANGE hooped, WHITE cap". https://www.skysports.com/racing/racecards/punchestown/23-11-2025/1347574/john-durkan-memorial-punchestown-chase-grade-1
- **"Green and gold hoops" is NOT a registered wording.** It is how the press and the Jockey Club describe them. https://www.thejockeyclub.co.uk/the-racing/racing-explained/jockeys/jockey-silks-and-colours/ ; https://www.sportinglife.com/racing/news/cobden-the-latest-top-jockey-in-the-green-and-gold-hoops/229957
- **How the second colour looks:** the same silks are registered as "yellow" in Britain and "orange" in Ireland and are called "gold" in the press, which points to a deep golden yellow rather than lemon. **I did not look at a photo, so the exact shade is unconfirmed.** Pick the shade from a race photo.
