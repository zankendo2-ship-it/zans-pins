## Where it is going wrong
1. Nearly half the feed is adverts. 56 of 114 posts were mug or bauble adverts and their median was 5 views. Brand-new accounts with no followers cannot grow on adverts.
2. Nothing earns a share or a save. 1 share and 0 saves in a week. Those are the signals that push a post beyond the first test audience.
3. The videos are slides. Text cards with a slow zoom and stat-heavy captions do not hold people for the first 2 seconds, so watch time is low.
4. Far too many posts. 114 in a week (16 a day) spreads thin effort across posts that each get a handful of views.
5. Instagram is barely being recommended (about 9 views a reel from 21 reels). Needs the in-app Account Status check. Likely causes: new account, long caption walls, 15 to 24 hashtags on older posts, betting footers, AI-illustration notes.
6. No conversation. We post and leave. No replies to other racing accounts, no comments on big racing posts, 10 comments received all week.
7. Timing with the racing calendar works, generic content does not: the two best posts ever rode Breeders' Cup weekend; the best Instagram and YouTube posts were "today at Aintree/Cheltenham" and trainer stats on the day.
8. TikTok has two removals and a warning to 27 Nov, and race-day posts (its best performers) are now off limits there.
9. Zero link clicks: links sit in captions where they cannot be tapped (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts).

## What works for us (small samples)
- Big-event timing (Breeders' Cup: 21,830 and 5,563 on TikTok).
- Trainer and course stats on the day of a meeting (Instagram 107, 31, 25; YouTube 228).
- Racing legends and nostalgia (TikTok 793, 773, 709, and our only share).
- A choice question on a gift post ("Which handle colour would you choose?": 41 likes, 3 comments, our most-engaged TikTok).

## Plan
1. Cut to 2 or 3 strong posts a day in total, not 16.
2. Ratio: 3 fan posts (legends, big-race build-ups, course cheat sheets, "which would you pick" questions) to 1 gift post.
3. Every video opens on motion and a spoken hook in the first second; 12 to 20 seconds; one idea.
4. Every post is built to be sent or saved: cheat sheets, "this or that" debates, quizzes.
5. Ride the calendar: Haydock big chase day 21 Nov, Newbury 28 Nov, Constitution Hill at Kempton, King George on 26 Dec.
6. Captions: 2 or 3 short lines and the question. No data-source lines, no music credits where the track is ours.
7. Spend 15 minutes a day replying and commenting on big racing accounts' posts (Alex or drafted for him).
8. Fix link paths: Instagram Story link sticker (added by hand), link in bio page with designer and Etsy, Pinterest pins straight to the listing, 5 fresh pins a day.
9. Measure each Monday: views, sends, saves, comments per post; keep what beats the median, drop what does not.
