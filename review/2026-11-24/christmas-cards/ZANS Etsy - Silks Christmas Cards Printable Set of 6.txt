Printable horse racing Christmas cards: a set of 6 jockey silks designs in bold racing colours (hoops, checks, diamonds and spots), as instant PDF downloads you print and fold at home into A5 cards.

Each design looks like a set of racing silks, with the top strip in the jockey's cap colour and MERRY CHRISTMAS across it. Two of the six also show the jersey and cap in a cream panel in the middle. Digital file only, nothing is posted.

- F: navy and white checks with the jersey in the middle, red cap

WHAT YOU GET
One zip file with 13 PDFs:

HOW TO PRINT
Print at actual size (100%) on white card, about 200-300 gsm, page 2 on the back of page 1, then fold in half. Print as many as you like for your own use. A home printer leaves a thin white edge; trim it off or take the file to a print shop for edge-to-edge colour.
