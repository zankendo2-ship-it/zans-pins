How to print your silks Christmas card
What is in the file
Page 1: the outside of the card - the back (left half) and the front (right half).
Page 2: the inside - blank on the left, your message page on the right.
A4 files fold to an A5 card (148 x 210 mm). US Letter files fold to 5.5 x 8.5 inches; trim along
the marks for a 5.5 x 7.8 inch card.
Printing
1. Use thin white card, 200-300 gsm, or good photo paper.
2. Open the PDF and choose the paper size that matches the file (A4 or US Letter), landscape.
3. Set the scale to Actual size / 100% - not "fit to page".
4. Print on both sides of the sheet (or print page 1, then put the sheet back in to print page 2 on
the reverse).
5. Test on plain paper first: the blank half must be behind the front of the card, and the message
page behind the back, both the right way up. If the inside comes out upside down, switch
between "flip on long edge" and "flip on short edge" (printers differ).
Finishing
1. Fold in half along the small mark at the top and bottom of the sheet.
2. US Letter: trim the thin white strip at the top and bottom along the small marks at the sides.
3. A home printer leaves a thin white edge. You can trim 3-4 mm off every side after folding for
edge-to-edge colour (the card will be a little smaller), or take the file to a print shop for a full-
size card.
