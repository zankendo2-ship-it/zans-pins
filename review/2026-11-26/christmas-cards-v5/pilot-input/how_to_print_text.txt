How to print your silks Christmas card
What is in the file
Page 1: the outside of the card - the back (left half) and the front (right half).
Page 2: the inside - blank on the left, your message page on the right. Please print it exactly as it is:
do not swap the halves.
A4 files fold to an A5 card (148 x 210 mm). US Letter files fold to 5.5 x 8.5 inches, then trim along
the marks for a 5.5 x 7.8 inch card.
Before you start
Open the download in a web browser or on a computer (not the Etsy app), then open the PDF in a
PDF reader.
Check the thickest paper your printer takes (it is in the printer manual, often 160-220 gsm for home
printers). Use white card up to that weight, ideally 200-300 gsm, or a print shop for thicker card.
Printing
1. Choose the paper size that matches the file (A4 or US Letter) and landscape.
2. Set the scale to Actual size / 100%, not "fit to page".
3. Two-sided printing: start with flip on short edge. If your printer has no two-sided setting, print
page 1, put the same sheet back in and print page 2 on the reverse.
4. Test on plain paper first. Fold the test sheet: the blank half must be behind the front of the card
and the message page behind the back, both the right way up. If the inside is upside down, change
to "flip on long edge" (printers differ) and test again.
Folding and trimming
1. Fold, don't cut, in the middle. Fold the sheet in half along the small marks at the top and bottom
edge.
2. US Letter only: trim the thin white strip at the top and bottom, along the small marks at the sides.
3. A home printer leaves a thin white edge round the outside. You can trim the outside edges only
(top, bottom and the open side) for edge-to-edge colour. This makes the card a few millimetres
smaller than the sizes above. Or take the file to a print shop for a full-size card.
