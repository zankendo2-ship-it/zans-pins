## Fix made in v5 to the PDFs themselves

The v4 PDFs were 297.0 x 210.2 mm (A4) and 279.7 x 216.2 mm (Letter): Chromium rounds page sizes up by up to 0.3 mm. A page bigger than the paper can make some printers shrink it. v5 sets every page box to the exact paper size: A4 landscape 297.0 x 210.0 mm, US Letter landscape 279.4 x 215.9 mm, How-to A4 portrait 210.0 x 297.0 mm. Only 0.2-0.3 mm of bleed at one edge is lost; no artwork moves or scales.
