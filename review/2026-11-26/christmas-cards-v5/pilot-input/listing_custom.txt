=== DESCRIPTION (3533 characters) ===
A racing silks Christmas card in your own jockey colours: a racing green card with your silks (jersey and cap) drawn in the middle, gold sparkles and MERRY CHRISTMAS in gold, sent to you as a printable PDF to print and fold at home.

We draw your silks from the colours and pattern you send, send you a picture to approve, then deliver your print-ready PDFs to your Etsy purchases when we complete your order. Digital file only, nothing is posted.

HOW TO ORDER
1. In the personalisation box, write your jersey pattern and colours, the sleeves and the cap colour, e.g. "red and white hoops, hoops on sleeves, black cap".
2. We draw your silks and send you a picture to check, usually within 1 working day of your order.
3. Once you approve it, we aim to send your PDFs within 1 working day. They appear in your Etsy purchases, ready to print.
Any pattern: plain, hoops, stripes, spots, checks, diamonds, chevrons, quartered, sash, cross belts, halves, seams and more.
For Christmas, please order by 18 December and approve your picture by 20 December, so there is time to print. Times can be longer if we need to ask about your design.

EVERY CARD HAS
- Front: racing green with a thin gold border, your silks big in the middle and MERRY CHRISTMAS in gold
- Inside: Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year, a space for your message and a small jersey in your silks
- Back: the ZANS Racing Christmas logo and Wishing you a Merry Christmas

WHAT YOU GET
- Your card on A4 (folds to A5, 148 x 210 mm, before any edge trimming) and on US Letter (folds to 5.5 x 8.5 in, then trim along the marks to 5.5 x 7.8 in), as PDFs
- A one-page How to print guide
This is a made-to-order digital file, so there is no instant download: your files arrive once we complete your order. Etsy downloads open in a web browser, not in the Etsy app.
Print as many copies as you like for your own use.

HOW TO PRINT
Print at actual size (100%), landscape, on white card (about 200-300 gsm, or the thickest your printer takes), page 2 on the back of page 1. Start with two-sided printing set to flip on short edge and test on plain paper first. Fold, don't cut, in the middle. A home printer leaves a thin white edge: you can trim the outside edges for edge-to-edge colour, which makes the card a few millimetres smaller, or take the file to a print shop. The How to print guide has every step.

WHO IT'S FOR
Racehorse owners, syndicate members and racing fans: send Christmas cards in your own colours, or give one in a friend's silks.

GOOD TO KNOW
- The silks in the photos are examples only
- We draw the colours and pattern only: no owner, horse or jockey names, logos or badges are added
- A card in a famous owner's colours does not mean any link with, or approval from, that owner
- We may decline a design we can't draw well or can't make
- Colours on paper can look slightly different from your screen
- For your own personal use and gifts only. Please don't resell, share or upload the files
- ZANS is independent and not connected with or approved by any racecourse, owner, jockey or trainer
- The card photos are digital mock-ups made by ZANS

RETURNS
As this is digital content made to your design, if you agree to us starting and sending your files before the 14-day cancellation period ends, and confirm that you then lose your right to cancel, that right ends once we send your files. If a file won't open or isn't as described, message us and we'll fix it or refund you. This doesn't affect your statutory rights.
