### Free stock libraries (checked on their official licence pages on 27 Nov 2026)
| Source | Commercial use | Credit needed | Key limits (quoted from the page) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pexels (pexels.com/license) | Yes, including ads and marketing | No | "Don't sell unaltered copies... on a physical product"; "Don't imply endorsement... by people or brands"; "Don't use... as part of your trade-mark... business name" |
| Pixabay (pixabay.com/service/license-summary) | Yes, with limits | No | Cannot sell or distribute content on its own; recognisable trademarks, logos or brands cannot be used commercially or printed on products for sale; no immoral or illegal use "especially Content which features recognisable people" |
| Unsplash (unsplash.com/license) | Yes | No (appreciated) | Cannot be compiled to build a competing image service. The page does not cover trademarks or recognisable people, so we treat them as not cleared |

How we use them: general mood only (countryside, Christmas lights, grass, sky). They can NEVER stand in for a named horse, a real race, a racecourse brand or a recognisable jockey. A stock photo of "a racehorse" is not the horse we name.

### Not yet verified (do not use until checked)
- Wikimedia Commons / public domain: the official reuse page could not be read in this check. Each file has its own licence (CC0, public domain or CC BY needing credit). Do not use until a thread has opened that file's own licence page and recorded licence, author and credit line.
- Pixabay music and sound: the licence summary lists music, but the music-specific terms were not read separately. Check before use.

### Music rules
- TikTok Commercial Music Library: free for business accounts INSIDE TikTok only. Its terms say "Commercial Uses outside of TikTok are not permitted". So the same video on Instagram, YouTube Shorts or Pinterest needs music that is licensed for those uses. Plan: on TikTok pick from the Commercial Music Library; for other platforms upload with a separate licensed track or the platform's own library.
- Never use chart music, YouTube or Spotify tracks.

### What to avoid
- Broadcaster, racecourse or news race footage and photos (Getty, PA, Racing Post, ITV, Sky and so on). Not free and not licensable here.
- Real jockeys' faces or identifiable people (our rule, and the stock licences above also limit it).
- Owners' names, and the Juddmonte silks (banned).
- Any real horse photo unless the rights holder has given written permission, or a file has a verified free licence.
- Race times, odds, tips, "can X win" language, and superlatives like "best".
