# Christmas cards: print list (joint fixes item 7)

Made 23 Nov 2026. Drafts; nothing listed or ordered.

All files are in `batch/2026-11-24/christmas-cards/`. Every front is 1500x2100 px, which is 5x7 in at 300 dpi, TRIM SIZE ONLY.

**Bleed: there is none yet.** The fronts were built to the trim size. A printer normally wants 3 mm (0.125 in) bleed on each edge, which makes a 5x7 front 1575x2175 px at 300 dpi, and wants text kept about 5 mm inside the trim. So before any proof is ordered each front must be re-rendered at the chosen product's own template size. I have not done that, because the card product (Printify or Printful, and which blueprint) has not been chosen and I cannot open either catalogue from this session. Cards that will need care when bleed is added:
- Full-photo cards (1, 2, 5, 8 to 18): the photo is stretched to cover, so adding bleed only needs a re-render; no content sits on the edge.
- Cards with metal strips at the very top and bottom (4, 6, 7, 19): the strips sit on the trim edge and would be partly cut. They must move inside the safe area or be made taller.
- Card 3 (cream border) and card 12 (red border): borders are even now and will look uneven if the cut drifts; widen them.
- Card 20: the red side bar runs to the left edge; extend it into the bleed.
Pictures are 1024x1440 FLUX output enlarged about 1.46 times to 1500x2100 (card 1 is a crop enlarged about 1.83 times), so they are softer than a true 300 dpi photo. A print proof is the only real test.
