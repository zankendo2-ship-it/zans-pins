# Christmas cards: print list (revised 24 Nov after joint verdict f8414d4)

Drafts. Nothing listed. The PNGs below are TRIM FRONTS (the front panel artwork only), not finished cards.

## Product
Printable DIGITAL card: the buyer downloads a PDF and prints it at home or at a print shop, then folds it in half.

## Sheet sizes (300 dpi)
| Sheet | Pixels | Half panel |
|---|---|---|
| A4 landscape | 3508x2480 | 1754x2480 |
| US Letter landscape | 3300x2550 | 1650x2550 |

## Layout
- Page 1 (outside): the FRONT is the right-hand half; the BACK is the left-hand half (small ZANS logo and website, low on the panel).
- Page 2 (inside): BLANK white, for the buyer's own message. No printed greeting inside.
- Fold: one vertical fold down the centre of the landscape sheet, so the card stands portrait. Small grey fold ticks sit at the very top and bottom edge only.
- The 5:7 front artwork is placed whole, centred in the right-hand panel with a 6 mm (71 px) safe margin, and is never stretched. On A4 it sits at 1612x2257 px; on US Letter at 1508x2111 px. The white margin is deliberate: home printers cannot print to the edge.
- No bleed.
- Print instructions for the buyer: print at "actual size / 100%" (not "fit to page"); for double-sided printing choose "flip on short edge"; card or heavy paper, 200 to 300 gsm.
- Picture quality: the pictures are 1024x1440 originals enlarged about 1.6 times on A4 (about 1.5 times on Letter), so they print slightly soft. Listings must not promise photo sharpness.
