1. TikTok could not tell it was a racing video. The tags were all gift tags (#horseracinggifts, #christmasbaubles...), with no #horseracing and no #ConstitutionHill, so it was shown to gift shoppers, not racing fans.
2. The caption read like an advert: the same bauble template as every other day, plus "Link in bio". Our posts with a shop pitch get 150 to 270. Our racing story posts get 650 to 800.
3. It was short and had nothing new in it (17 s, no fact or story), and it went out at 18:00 on a Friday, our weakest slot. The Ombudsman bauble got 682 in the same format because its first line was news.
