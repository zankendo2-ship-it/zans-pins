What Does the Going Mean in Horse Racing?

The going is how firm or soft the ground is. It is announced before racing and can change during the day, and it is one of the first things to check on a racecard, because many horses run much better on one type of ground than another.

The going scale from firm to heavy

GoingWhat it means
FirmVery fast, dry ground. Rare in Britain and Ireland and often watered to avoid it.
Good to firmFast ground with a little give. Common in summer Flat racing.
GoodThe middle of the scale: a horse leaves a light hoof print.
Good to softSome give in the ground; times get slower.
SoftWet ground; hooves sink in and stamina starts to matter more.
HeavyVery wet, testing ground. Races become a test of stamina and slow right down.

In Ireland you will also see yielding, which sits between good and soft. A course can also give two descriptions, such as “good, good to soft in places”, when the ground varies around the track.

Good to soft, soft and heavy: what changes

As the ground gets softer, races are run more slowly and take more out of the horses. Horses with plenty of stamina, or with big feet and a round action, often cope better. Horses that rely on pure speed can struggle, especially on heavy ground.

All-weather going: standard, slow and fast

All-weather tracks race on a man-made surface such as Polytrack or Tapeta, so they are not described as good or soft. Their going is usually standard, sometimes standard to slow or slow, and occasionally standard to fast or fast.

How the going is measured

The clerk of the course walks the track and decides the official going. Many British courses also publish a GoingStick reading, a number from a tool pushed into the turf to measure how firm it is. Rain, watering, sun and wind can all change the going during a meeting, so check for updates on the day.

Why some horses need a certain ground

Every horse is different. Some only show their best on fast ground, others love it soft. The simplest check is the horse's past form: how has it run when the going was the same as today?
