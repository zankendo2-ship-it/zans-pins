All 14 Breeders' Cup races in UK time

Friday's five juvenile races start at 18:55 UK time and Saturday's nine run from 17:10 to the Breeders' Cup Classic at 22:16. The full list is in our free article: Breeders' Cup 2026 UK times.

Main contenders in every race

Each race page picks out the main contenders and explains why, in plain English in the Beginner edition and in full detail in Advanced and Pro.

Every runner with its colours, trainer and jockey

Every runner is shown in its own racing colours, drawn by ZANS, with its trainer and jockey, so you can follow each horse as the race unfolds.

European raiders: grass or dirt?

By our own count of the Breeders' Cup from 2011 to 2025, European-trained horses won 48 of the 98 turf races and none of the 105 dirt races. The guide shows where each overseas runner fits.

Beginner, Advanced or Pro: which edition?

Beginner (bronze) if the Breeders' Cup is new to you. Advanced (silver) for contenders, post positions and the overseas raiders. Pro (gold) for a full-field read of every race and how our figures were tested.
