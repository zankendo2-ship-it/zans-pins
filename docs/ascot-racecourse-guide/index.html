The Ascot course map: straight mile and round course

Five, six and seven furlong races and one of the two mile starts use the straight course; 1m2f, 1m4f, 1m6f and 2m races go right-handed round the course, past Swinley Bottom, into a short rising straight of about 2½ furlongs. Every edition opens with a ZANS-drawn course map showing where each distance starts.
