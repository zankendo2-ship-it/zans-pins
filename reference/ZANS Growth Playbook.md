# ZANS Growth Playbook (followers, likes, comments, shares)
Written 20 Nov 2026 (updated 09:35 UTC: no added moving openers) for the ZANS ETSY & SOCIAL chat. Read with "ZANS Social Caption Playbook.md". Every ZANS rule still applies:
Alex approves every post, TikTok safety, no tips or winner claims, no paper-test record lines, posting caps, our voice, no filming by Alex.

## 1. What our own numbers say (Metricool, 10-19 Nov)
- Instagram Reels: 75-100% of viewers skip in the first 3 seconds, average watch 1-5 s, 0 shares, 0 comments, 0-31 views. Reels are
  being shown to a few people and dropped at once. The opening second is the whole problem.
- TikTok: videos stop at about 200-800 views with 0-1 shares and 0-2 comments. Photo posts get about 240 views. A 124 s video got 272.
- What does well is the TOPIC: famous names and broad subjects (Legends in Numbers: Flat greats 798, Grand National 711; Ombudsman
  bauble 678). Niche horses on their own get 189-239.
- Followers: TikTok about 76, IG 47. Website visits from TikTok/IG: none.

## 2. How the networks rank posts now (2026 consensus of platform statements + industry guides)
- TikTok: tests each video with a small batch. It widens only when the batch watches to the end, rewatches, SHARES, saves and comments.
  Shares and completion beat likes. Small accounts can break out on any single video, so every video must be built to pass the test batch.
- Instagram Reels: Instagram says "sends per reach" (people sending the Reel by DM) is the strongest signal for reaching non-followers,
  then watch time, then likes. Use Trial Reels (shown to non-followers first) for new formats. Originality matters: no watermarks, no
  reposted clips.
- YouTube Shorts: ranked on "viewed v swiped away" plus watch-through. A clear title with the subject first helps search and the Shorts feed.
- Facebook Reels: same as Instagram, with more weight on comments and shares among over-35s (good for racing fans).
- Pinterest: a search engine. Keyword-first titles and descriptions, a fresh pin for every video, steady posting. Clicks to the shop come from here.
- X: replies and quote posts drive reach. Join live racing conversations on big days (Betfair Chase Sat 21 Nov) with a short clip.
- LinkedIn: business story angle only (building a racing gift brand), 1-2 a week.

## 3. The six levers, in order of impact
1. The first second. Hook on screen by 1 s, nothing static, no logo first. NEW videos are built with motion from the start. Do NOT add a
   separate 'moving opener' clip to existing/finished videos (Alex 20 Nov: "those videos look awful!! Leave them as they are").
2. Famous-name topics. Build every post around a name or event fans already search for (Constitution Hill, Frankel, Grand National,
   Cheltenham, Betfair Chase, King George, Boxing Day). Put niche horses and products inside that bigger story.
3. A share reason. Every post has a "send this to..." reason: a debate (A or B?), a ranking, a "tag the fan who...", a nostalgia moment,
   a Christmas gift idea for a racing fan. Shares and DM sends are the strongest signals on TikTok and Instagram.
4. A comment question. One short question on screen, the same question in the caption. Pin our own first comment with it.
5. Length. 7-20 s for products and face-offs, made to loop (the last frame leads back to the first). 20-35 s only for "Legends in
   Numbers"-style lists, which hold attention.
6. Reply to every comment within the first hour, and reply to some with a video reply on TikTok/IG (cheap new posts that rank well).

## 4. Growth routines (no filming needed)
- Series: run named, repeating series so people follow for the next one: "Legends in Numbers" (proven), "Who wins? A or B" face-offs,
  "Racing on this day", "Guess the horse from its silks", "Christmas gifts for racing fans" (gift posts only, no prices claimed as deals).
- Trends: check the TikTok/IG trending sounds each morning. Offer Alex 3 options, he picks (music rule). Use trending formats with our content.
- Engage outward: 10-15 genuine comments a day on bigger UK racing and equine accounts and on big-race posts (written by us for Alex to
  post if a network needs his thumb). Never spam links.
- Collabs: Instagram Collab posts with small racing/equine fan accounts and racing gift makers who agree. Alex approves each partner.
- Cross-post every video as a TikTok, Reel, YouTube Short (title keyword first, max 3 hashtags incl. #Shorts) and a Pinterest video pin.
- Big days: plan 2-3 posts around each big race (preview topic, live-day fan moment, next-day "what a race"). TikTok gets only the safe
  fan/history version.
- Hashtags: 3-5 relevant ones (#horseracing #constitutionhill #cheltenham etc.), no hashtag walls. Keywords in the caption matter more.
- Best times: use Metricool getBestTimeToPostByNetwork weekly; keep the same daily slot per series.
- Measure every post at 24 h: views, average watch, skip rate, shares, comments. Write it in views_log.csv. Stop formats that miss twice,
  repeat formats that beat the median.

## 5. STANDING CHECKLIST - every post must pass before it goes to review
[ ] Hook text on screen by 1 s; no logo first; NEVER bolt a separate moving opener onto a finished video (Alex rejected these 20 Nov)
[ ] Built on a famous name, big race or broad topic (niche horse only inside that story)
[ ] Clear share reason (debate, ranking, nostalgia, tag a fan, gift idea)
[ ] One short question on screen = the same question in the caption; first comment pinned
[ ] Length 7-20 s (lists up to 35 s), loops cleanly
[ ] Trending sound chosen from 3 options Alex picked; our Gemini voice where there is a voiceover; no sound effects
[ ] Caption: keyword first line, 3-5 hashtags, no banned lines ("helping you try and find a winner"), no "AI illustration" text
[ ] ZANS branding, centred text, course colour on race videos, www.zanshorseracing.co.uk on the last slide
[ ] Facts checked in 2 sources: horse still racing and in that code, silks, coat, jockey; real current rivals in face-offs
[ ] No tips, no winner or profit claims, no paper-test record lines, no freebies, no fake reviews; 18+ footer on betting content only
[ ] TikTok version: no betting, tips, race times, race-day or win records; max 2 a day; independent check
[ ] Cross-post plan: Reel, TikTok (safe version), YouTube Short title, Pinterest pin title (<=100 chars, keyword first) + description
[ ] Within the daily caps per network; not a repeat of anything already posted (check posted history)
[ ] Reply plan: someone answers comments in the first hour
[ ] Alex has approved it
