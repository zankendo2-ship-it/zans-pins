# ZANS TikTok Rulebook (approved by Alex 21 Nov 2026)
Built from Metricool, every TikTok 10-21 Nov 2026. Racing story/news/history posts of 24-67 s got 665-801 views. Every product post
(baubles 14-18 s at 18:00, gift-only tags, template caption; mug photos) got 150-270. Ombudsman bauble got 682 because it had a news hook.
This sits alongside the ZANS Growth Playbook. Every TikTok must pass every box below before its REVIEW REQUEST, and Alex says yes to each post.

## Pre-post checklist (every box ticked)
[ ] MIX: at least 2 in every 3 TikToks are racing story, news or history about a famous horse or race. The product (bauble/mug) appears only as a 2-3 s end card.
[ ] HOOK: first line and on-screen hook (by 1 s) = news or a strong fact (retirement, comeback, record, number). Never a vague question ("Is this the most popular horse ever?").
[ ] NEWS FAST: news posts go out the same day the news breaks.
[ ] HASHTAGS: #horseracing + the horse's name + 1-3 topic tags (e.g. #jumpracing #cheltenham). Max 5. Never gift-only tags on TikTok; gift tags belong on Etsy and Pinterest.
[ ] CAPTION: one hook line + one question + tags. No repeated template text, no shop pitch, no "Link in bio" sales line (the bio already has the link).
[ ] LENGTH: 20-35 s, built on a story or numbers (Legends in Numbers style). No 14-18 s product loops.
[ ] TIME: post at 12:00-13:00 or 17:00 UK (not 18:00). Use Sundays. Log every post in views_log.csv for the time test.
[ ] REPLIES: someone replies to every comment within the first hour; our own question comment pinned.
[ ] STRIKE-SAFE (two strikes 6 + 13 Nov): no betting, tips, odds, race times, race-day previews, win records or "find a winner" lines. Fan/history versions only. Independent check. Max 2 TikToks a day, 1080x1920.
[ ] FACTS: horse still racing and in that code, silks, coat and jockey checked in 2 sources; real current rivals in face-offs.
[ ] ALL ZANS RULES: our Gemini voice, 3 music options for Alex, no sound effects, no "AI illustration" text, no freebies, no fake reviews, no paper-test record lines.
[ ] ALEX: approved this exact post.

## Daily 21:00 check
Every evening a scheduled check reads that day's views per network in Metricool and marks any post under par (TikTok under 500 views;
other networks under that network's 14-day median). For each one it gives the likely reason against this checklist and a fix for the next
day. Alex only hears about it when a fix is needed.
