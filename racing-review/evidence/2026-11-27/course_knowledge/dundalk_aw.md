## 1. Course profile

From our hand-written profile (`config/course_profiles/dundalk.json`, our own words), kept to what applies to all-weather flat:

- **Direction:** Left-handed
- **Surface:** Polytrack
- **Circuit:** A flat, fair oval of about 1m2f
- **Home straight:** About 2½f
- **Sprints:** 5f starts in a chute, so the minimum trip does not start on a bend
- **Character:** Often rides fast

**Layout**
- A chute means five-furlong races start on the straight rather than on a bend.

**Tactics**
- Guides report low draws having an edge at all trips and prominent racers doing well over five to seven furlongs; the ZANS figures below test both claims.
- The surface often rides fast, which can be against horses that prefer slow ground on turf.

**Ground**
- Synthetic Polytrack; going reads standard or similar.

**Guide note:** Low draws and prominent racers are said to be favoured, especially from 5f to 7f.

- Season: all year, with many winter evening meetings

- **ZANS stiffness indicator** (our standard times v the median UK/IRE track, same trips): no standard times yet
