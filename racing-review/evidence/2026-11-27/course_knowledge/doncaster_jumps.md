## 1. Course profile

From our hand-written profile (`config/course_profiles/doncaster.json`, our own words), kept to what applies to jumps:

- **Direction:** Left-handed, flat and jumps
- **Character:** Very galloping and fair, basically flat
- **Ground:** Free-draining; usually good or quicker
- **Surface:** Turf

**Layout**
- A left-handed, galloping and almost level track; the only real gradient is a gentle climb over Rose Hill around ten furlongs out.

**Tactics**
- One of the fairest tracks in Britain: form from Doncaster tends to hold up well elsewhere.
- Horses need to be good and genuine to win, but the course itself sets few traps.

**Ground**
- Good drainage means the ground is often good or quicker, and runners usually see their races out.

- Season: flat from March to November, jumps in the winter
