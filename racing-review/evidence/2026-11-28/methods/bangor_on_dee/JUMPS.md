# Bangor-on-Dee JUMPS - ZANS method v1.0 (DATA-BUILT, 26 Oct 2026)

No method from the owner on file. Built from our history: 599 Bangor jumps races 2019-26 (380 since 2022).
Only rules that agree in BOTH periods (2019-21, worked out as full minus since-2022, and 2022-26) are used.
Tables: reports/methods/bangor_on_dee_JUMPS_rules.md (2019+) and ..._rules_since2022.md.

## Rules used
- Ground specialist (won on today's exact going, only 1-2 in the field have) x1.05: A/E 1.18 (266), ~1.22 / 1.16.
- Improver (last RPR above its previous best) x1.03: A/E 1.11 (596), ~1.22 / 1.06.
- Won last time x1.03: A/E 1.07 (508), ~1.07 / 1.07.
- Top recent RPR: national jumps default (x1.05) kept - Bangor 1.09 (~1.04 / 1.12), in line with national 1.12.

## Tested, NOT used (flip between periods or too weak)
- Course & distance winner 0.81 (~1.00 / 0.72); course winner 0.95 (~1.11 / 0.88).
- Exact-conditions RPR 1.02 (~0.89 / 1.09); unproven trip 1.10 (~1.01 / 1.15).
- Usual front-runner 0.86 (~0.65 / 0.97); lone front-runner 1.04 (~0.98 / 1.07); held up 1.06 (~1.00 / 1.10).
- Jockey under 6% 1.02 (~1.17 / 0.90); keen/hung 1.09 (~1.14 / 1.07) - no 'keen' rule kind yet.
