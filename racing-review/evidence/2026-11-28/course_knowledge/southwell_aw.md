## 1. Course profile

From our hand-written profile (`config/course_profiles/southwell_aw_flat.json`, our own words), kept to what applies to all-weather flat:

- **Direction:** Left-handed oval
- **Character:** Flat, fair, and compared by jockeys to an American dirt track
- **Circuit:** About 1m2f round, with a home straight of about 3f
- **Straight course:** 5f races (and the 1m6f start) use a straight chute before the final bend
- **Surface:** Tapeta, which replaced the old Fibresand surface
- **Starts:** 1m2f to 1m5f in front of the stands; 6f, 7f and 1m on the back straight

**Layout**
- Five-furlong races use a straight course, so they are run without a bend; the 1m6f start also sits on that chute before the runners join the oval.
- The final turn is on the tight side by British standards, a little more sweeping than a typical American dirt track.

**Tactics**
- With a flat track and a fair surface, few horses are beaten by the course itself; racing position into the final turn can matter on the tighter bends.
- Straight 5f races and round-course races call for different things, so read the sprint figures separately from the round-course figures.
- Tapeta can ride on the slow side, which can hold times down; a moderate time here is not always a weak performance.

**Ground**
- An artificial Tapeta surface, so the going is described as standard, standard to slow or similar rather than good or soft.
- The surface changed from Fibresand to Tapeta; ZANS figures here only use races run on Tapeta, so older Fibresand form does not mix in.

**Guide note:** Course guides describe Tapeta tracks as fair and note that official ratings earned here can come out a few pounds low because times are slower; that is a guide view, not a ZANS measurement.

- Season: all year round

- **ZANS stiffness indicator** (our standard times v the median UK/IRE track, same trips): All-weather flat: +0.2% against the median UK/IRE track over the same 5 trip(s)
