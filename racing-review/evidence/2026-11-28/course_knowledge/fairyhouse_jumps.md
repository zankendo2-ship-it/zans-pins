## 1. Course profile

From our hand-written profile (`config/course_profiles/fairyhouse.json`, our own words), kept to what applies to jumps:

- **Direction:** Right-handed
- **Shape:** Square, about 1m6½f round
- **Character:** Wide and galloping on the flat
- **Jumps:** Eleven fences; eight hurdles
- **Surface:** Turf

**Layout**
- A right-handed, square-shaped track of about a mile and six and a half furlongs in County Meath, with a run-in of almost three furlongs and a slight rise to the finish.

**Tactics**
- A wide, galloping and fair track that slightly favours horses racing prominently.

**Ground**
- Turf; spring and winter going can be soft.

- Season: through the year, including the Irish Grand National at Easter
