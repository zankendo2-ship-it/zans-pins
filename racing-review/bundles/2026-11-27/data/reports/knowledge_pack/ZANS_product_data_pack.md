## 3. How the picks are made (model v10)

- Win pick = conditional-logit model on 46 evidence features plus the market price, flat and jumps fitted separately, then each course's method overlay (rule factors tested on history in two periods).
- Confidence 0-10 is a calibrated win chance. BET label = confidence 8+ AND the evidence-only model agrees.
- Features: rpr_b3, rpr_miss, spd_b3, spd_miss, rpr_last, or_rel, or_miss, wgt, fresh, rest, long, debut, runs, cd_course, cd_dist, won_last, placed_last, trainer_sr, trainer_14d, jockey_sr, pace_suits, pace_against, spd_trend, going_fit, going_miss, trip_rpr, trip_spd, course_placed, trainer_course, jockey_course, draw_bias, pace_bias, jockey_14d, trainer_jockey, won_at_weight, class_drop, class_rise, weight_bias, new_trainer, rpr_cb7, jump_mistake_lto, fell_lto, ft_visor_jumps, hg_removed_flat, mare_v_geldings, mark_gap

### Bet slip rules in force
- Win singles + doubles/trebles/Lucky 15/63 from 7/10+ win picks (any price from 1.00, trial since 20 Oct).
- Kept off every bet: 90+ days off; maiden/novice/beginners/bumper jumps; drifted 15%+; latest run missing or abroad (data gap); never run at the trip (and on going, jumps); Southwell AW handicaps; Fairyhouse jumps non-handicaps; heavy ground flat picks never on soft/heavy = single only (trial).
- Each-way singles and forecasts OFF; high-odds each-way Lucky 15 (12.00+, Sky Bet) ON; spread multiples.
- BET NOW emails: early value horses 15.00+ where Sky Bet pays EXTRA places.
