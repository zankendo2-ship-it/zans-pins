# Champions Day (Ascot) - what 8 years of results say

Owner 2026-10-09: going to Champions Day (Sat 17 Oct 2026). Source: our results history (Racing Post results data), Champions Day
2018-2025, the 6 British races (Long Distance Cup, Sprint, Fillies & Mares, QEII, Champion Stakes, Balmoral Handicap) = 48 races.
Script: reports/research/scripts/champions_day_history.py. These are past results, not a promise for this year.

## Whole day
- Favourites won 16 of 48 (33%). 19 winners were 10/1+ (11.00+), 10 were 20/1+.
- Our "top-rated" (best RPR in the last 12 months) won 13 of 48; a top-3 rated horse won 25 of 48.
- Ground: 42 of 48 races were on good to soft or softer (25 soft, 14 good to soft, 3 heavy); 2025 was good. On the soft days,
  23 of 42 winners had already won on soft or heavy.
- Winners' ages: 3yo 19, 4yo 13, 5yo 6, 6yo 8, 7yo 2.
- 20 of 48 winners won their last race. Most winners had run 3-8 weeks before (median about 5 weeks).
- Trainers with most wins: Gosden yard 7 (John Gosden 4, John & Thady Gosden 3), Roger Varian 5, Aidan O'Brien 4, William Haggas 4, Andrew Balding 3, Alan King 3.
- French-trained winners: The Revenant (2020), Sealiway (2021), Big Rock (2023), Calandagan (2025) - 4 of the 24 Group 1s since 2020 (Long Distance Cup is Group 2).

## What this means for the day
- The Long Distance Cup and the Fillies & Mares are where the market and our ratings are most reliable.
- Sprint, QEII and Balmoral are lottery-type races in these 8 years: small stakes or each-way only.
- Soft-ground proof matters most if it rains (it has been soft or good to soft in 7 of the 8 years).
- The full horse-by-horse study (ground, trip, course, mark, trainer, jockey, travel) gets built when the fields are declared
  (Thu 15 Oct, 2 days before).
