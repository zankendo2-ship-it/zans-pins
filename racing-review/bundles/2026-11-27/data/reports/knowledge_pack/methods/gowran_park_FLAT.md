# Gowran Park FLAT - ZANS method v1.0 DATA-BUILT (18 Oct 2026)

The owner has no Gowran Park flat method, so this one is built from our Gowran Park flat history: 720 races from 2019,
with a split check since 2022 (reports/methods/gowran_park_FLAT_rules.md). Only rules that point the same way in both
periods are used. Provisional until live cards confirm it.

| Rule | Factor | Gowran Park flat A/E (2019+ / since 2022) |
|---|---|---|
| Drawn 1-4 on good or firmer | x1.05 | 1.15 / 1.14 |
| Top recent RPR | x1.04 | 1.10 / 1.05 |
| Usual front-runner | x1.03 | 1.08 / 1.12 |
| Age 6+ no win/2nd in last 3 | x0.94 | 0.86 / 0.87 |
| Weakened / no extra in either of last 2 | x0.97 | 0.94 / 0.95 |

Not used (flip between periods or no edge): exact-conditions RPR (0.90 / 0.81 here but higher in 2019-21), improver
(1.06 / 0.99), won last (1.03 / 0.97), jockey under 6% (1.06 / 1.15 = no penalty), slow starters up to 1m (0.77 / 1.01),
jumps-bred stamina on yielding or softer (1.10 / 1.20, flips in 2019-21), held up (0.95 / 1.00), course winner (1.05 / 1.04, small).
Favourites A/E 1.11 / 1.02 - never opposed on price alone.

On Yielding To Soft or Soft, stalls 1-4 are below par in both periods (0.87 with 207 runners, 0.90 with 377), so the rule must not spread to softer ground. The 2019-21 yielding sample is small (19 winners), and today the factor would not have changed a single pick.
