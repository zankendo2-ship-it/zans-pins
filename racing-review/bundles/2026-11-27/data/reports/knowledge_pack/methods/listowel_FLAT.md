**What the winners on 31 May actually had in common:** all 7 were drawn **2–5**, and 5 of 7
raced prominently or led. 3 of the 7 were favourites. That's one card, so it is only a note;
the 7f draw figures above say the market already prices low draws.

## 3. Why the card went the way it did

- The method **picked the favourite in 5 of 7 races**, and 3 of those lost. That is normal at
  Listowel: favourites win about a third of races.
- The two lessons the chat drew were **both wrong on the facts**:
  - "Stamina Crossover / holding ground": the ground was Good.
  - "Value Inversion hurt Ballyadam": he won at evens as the RPR pick.
- The real miss: the method has no rule for **start risk** or **ground specialists**, which
  are the two strongest Listowel signals in 7 years of data.

## 4. ZANS METHOD v14.2 — LISTOWEL — FLAT TURF (current method)

```
ZANS METHOD v14.2 — LISTOWEL — FLAT TURF
Replaces AURORA-X v14.1 / North Kerry v2.1 (review 2, 31 May 2026). Evidence: reports/methods/
listowel_FLAT_rules.md (218 Listowel flat races 2019-2025) + reports/track_stats.

WIN PICK (price-free, owner rule): ZANS win model (all evidence: ratings, speed, trip, going,
  course form, draw/pace/weight DNA, trainer/jockey form + course records, class move, weight),
  then the Listowel overlay below. Price is never used for the win pick.

TRACK: left-handed, tight, inner rectangular circuit just over 1m, outer ~1m1f, 7f/1m chute,
  short run-in (~2f). Position and a clean start matter.

LISTOWEL OVERLAY (multipliers on the model's chance; ACTIVE = passed the data test):
  ACTIVE  Front-runner / lone front-runner, 6.5f-1m4f ........ x1.10 (lone leader x1.15)
          (A/E 1.36 / 1.42 here). NOT applied at 1m5f+ (1m6f leans to hold-up).
  ACTIVE  Start risk: slow away in 2+ of last 4, race up to 1m ... x0.70 (A/E 0.55, -70%)
  ACTIVE  Ground specialist: won on today's exact going and only
          1-2 runners in the field have ........................ x1.15 (A/E 1.58)
  ACTIVE  Top exact-conditions RPR (trip +-1f, same going) ...... x1.05; Sep-Nov x1.08
  SOFT    Usually held up, race up to 7.5f, <2 front-runners ..... x0.95
  SOFT    Weakened / no extra in either of last 2 runs .......... x0.97
  SOFT    Course & distance winner .............................. x1.03
  NOTE    Draw: show stall third + course draw table (7f low draw wins more, but is priced
          in: no multiplier). Jumps horse on flat 1m+ soft (A/E 1.03), keen/hung (1.05),
          improver score: shown as notes, no multiplier.
  DROPPED Bend Control Law (drawn 7+ at 7f, A/E 1.10), low draw on good/firm (0.90),
          Apprentice First-Time Trap (no edge), Value Inversion / favourite knock-out
          (Listowel favourites A/E 1.20), temperament reject.

RPR PICK: highest RPR horse proven at today's trip (+-1f) and going, moving down the list until
  one qualifies (owner's RPR rule); weight check vs last run.
LONGSHOT: 14/1+ at the morning price, 6+ runners: our best-ranked runner in that band
  (STRONG if in our top 3, else SPECULATIVE).
CONFIDENCE / BET: calibrated model chance -> 1-10; BET at 8+.
DATA GAPS: any horse with missing recent runs is labelled DATA GAP; no pace/anchor claims.

MIXED CARDS (e.g. September festival): each race uses the method for its own code:
  FLAT -> this file; HURDLE / CHASE / NHF -> methods/listowel/<CODE>.md.

REVIEW (nightly, automatic): official results + in-running comments; a rule changes only on the
  promotion test (30+ flagged runners at the course or 300+ nationally, A/E >= 1.05 in an
  earlier AND a later period to promote; <= 0.95 to drop). Never from one race or card.
```
