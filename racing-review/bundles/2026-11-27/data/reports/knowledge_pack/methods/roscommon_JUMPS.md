# Roscommon — JUMPS — ZANS method v1.0 (DATA-BUILT, no owner method)

Built 2026-09-27 at the owner's request ("I haven't got a method or review for Roscommon jumps - study and create one").
Evidence: rule test `reports/methods/roscommon_JUMPS_rules.md` (192 races since 2019, UK/IRE history only), split check
2019-21 / 2022-26, course pace tables (`reports/track_stats/pace_bias.csv`). No owner cards to score yet.

## 1. Course DNA (from our tables)
- Right-handed, tight ~1m2f circuit, uphill finish; a small summer/autumn jumps programme (192 races in 7 years = small samples).
- Pace: hurdles at 2m and 2m4f favour FRONT-RUNNERS vs the national average; 1m7½f leans to hold-ups (small sample);
  chases/bumpers: no clear bias. The daily pace map uses these tables per trip.

## 3. ZANS method v1.0 (from 28 Sep 2026)
Joint model (price + evidence) picks; BET only with EVIDENCE AGREES + confidence 8+. Angles: lone front-runner 1.06,
ground specialist 1.05. Cautions: jockey under 6% 0.85, weak finisher 0.90, C&D winner 0.93. All-course jumps rule:
never run on soft/heavy 0.86 when the ground is soft or heavy. Reviewed after every Roscommon card (learning loop).
