# Plumpton JUMPS - ZANS method v1.0 DATA-BUILT (18 Oct 2026)

The owner has no Plumpton method, so this one is built from our Plumpton jumps history: 730 races from 2019, with a split
check since 2022 (reports/methods/plumpton_JUMPS_rules.md). Only rules that point the same way in both periods are used.
Provisional until live cards confirm it.

| Rule | Factor | Plumpton A/E (2019+ / since 2022) |
|---|---|---|
| Top recent RPR | x1.08 | 1.25 / 1.19 (+7% at SP over the full period) |
| First time at the trip | x0.95 | 0.90 / 0.90 |
| Won on today's exact going (only 1-2 in the field have) | x1.03 | 1.09 / 1.11 |
| Usual front-runner | x1.03 | 1.11 / 1.05 |
| Improver (last RPR a new best) | x1.02 | 1.14 / 1.02 |

Not used (flip between periods or no edge): usually held up (0.96 / 0.82, higher in 2019-21), lone front-runner (1.06 / 0.87),
jockey under 6% (1.06 / 0.90), exact-conditions RPR (1.01 / 1.01), course winner (0.97 / 0.98), age 6+ out of form (1.00 / 1.02),
weakened last 2 (0.99 / 0.99). Favourites A/E 1.09 / 1.07 - never opposed on price alone.
