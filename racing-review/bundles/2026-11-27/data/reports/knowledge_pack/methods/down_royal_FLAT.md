## 1. Course DNA (from our tables)
- Right-handed, galloping ~1m7f circuit with a stiff finish (Northern Ireland; flat meetings mostly summer).
- Draw: no meaningful bias at 5f or 1m2½f; slight LOW advantage at 7f on good; at 7f on soft the high draw did better
  (small sample). Low draw 1-4 on good or firmer overall A/E 0.82 (597) - the daily draw tables handle this per trip.
- Pace: 5f and 7f on good lean to HOLD-UP horses (leaders struggle vs national); 1m2½f leans to hold-ups; 7f on soft
  favours front-runners (small sample).
