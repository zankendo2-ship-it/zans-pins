## 1. Course profile

From our hand-written profile (`config/course_profiles/musselburgh.json`, our own words), kept to what applies to flat (turf):

- **Direction:** Right-handed
- **Character:** Sharp, with two tight turns linking two long straights
- **Straight course:** Sprints run on a straight course
- **Ground:** Excellent drainage; rarely very testing
- **Surface:** Turf

**Layout**
- A sharp, right-handed track near Edinburgh where two tight turns link the back and home straights; the course was reconfigured to ease what was once Britain's tightest bend.

**Tactics**
- Balance matters, as at Chester; speedy horses that stay the trip usually do better than big gallopers on the round course.
- A good turn of foot can open up a winning gap quickly.
- Guides favour low draws on the round course up to about nine furlongs, and high draws on the straight course where fields group on the stands' rail.

**Ground**
- Good drainage keeps the going on the fast side, which helps speedier types.

**Guide note:** Low draws on the round course and high draws (stands' rail) on the straight course are the guide view; our draw figures check both.

- Season: all year: flat and jumps

- **ZANS stiffness indicator** (our standard times v the median UK/IRE track, same trips): Flat: -1.7% against the median UK/IRE track over the same 7 trip(s)
