## 1. Course profile

From our hand-written profile (`config/course_profiles/doncaster.json`, our own words), kept to what applies to flat (turf):

- **Character:** Very galloping and fair, basically flat
- **Gradient:** A slight rise (Rose Hill) about 10f from home
- **Straight mile:** 5f to 1m run on the straight course (the Lincoln is run here); a separate round-course mile starts in a chute
- **Ground:** Free-draining; usually good or quicker
- **Surface:** Turf

**Layout**
- A left-handed, galloping and almost level track; the only real gradient is a gentle climb over Rose Hill around ten furlongs out.
- All races from five furlongs to a mile can be run on the straight course, with a separate one-mile start on the round course in a chute.

**Tactics**
- One of the fairest tracks in Britain: form from Doncaster tends to hold up well elsewhere.
- On the straight course there is no strong side bias; winners come from the far side, the stands' rail and the middle.
- Horses need to be good and genuine to win, but the course itself sets few traps.

**Ground**
- Good drainage means the ground is often good or quicker, and runners usually see their races out.

- Season: flat from March to November, jumps in the winter

- **ZANS stiffness indicator** (our standard times v the median UK/IRE track, same trips): Flat: -0.6% against the median UK/IRE track over the same 9 trip(s)
