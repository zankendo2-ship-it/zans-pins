# When to place the bet on our WIN selections (TEST, 3 Nov 2026)

Owner 3 Nov: "Have you worked out when's best to place the bets on our win selections - morning, afternoon, the day before?"

Data: every locked race-day win pick 26 Sep - 2 Nov (first d0/race-morning folder each day, not superseded), the price
of that horse at each saved check (Sky Bet where listed, else best bookmaker), the SP, £10 win, non-runners out.
Scripts + full outputs: reports/research/scripts/timing_2026-11-03/ (t.py / t_out.txt, t2.py / t2_out.txt).

## 1. Our 7/10+ race-day picks, same 367 horses priced at every check

| When you bet | £10 win P/L |
|---|---|
| Night before (~17:00 UTC check) | +£394.30 - but see 2: you do not know the race-day pick yet |
| 09:30 race morning (our locked pick) | +£8.70 |
| Noon | -£38.30 |
| Starting price | -£72.80 |
| **09:30 with Best Odds Guaranteed** | **+£234.70** |
| Noon with BOG | +£140.50 |

09:30 + BOG beats noon and SP in both halves (26 Sep-14 Oct +£359 v SP +£174; 15 Oct-2 Nov -£125 v SP -£247).

## 2. Betting the night before = betting the DAY-BEFORE pick (what we really have then)

- 305 day-before 7/10+ picks: 72 (24%) were NOT our race-day pick next morning (later news: non-runners, going, prices).
  Those replaced picks won 14 of 72 and lost heavily (-£253.50 at the night-before price).
- The 169 that stayed 7/10+ picks and were priced both times: night-before price -£69 (BOG +£34) v 09:30 -£124 (BOG -£13);
  the night price was bigger 80 times, smaller 67 (both halves a little better at night: +£30, +£25).
- All day-before 7/10+ picks bet at the night-before price: 110/261, -£375.00. Our race-day 7/10+ picks at 09:30: 223/461,
  -£132.20 (BOG +£234.70 on the 367 priced at every check).
=> The small price gain the night before is wiped out by the 1-in-4 picks that change by morning.

## Answer
- WIN selections: bet at the 09:30 locked pack, with Best Odds Guaranteed (Sky Bet Club). Not the night before, not noon,
  not at the off.
- VALUE / each-way selections are the exception: bet at the first bookmaker price (BET NOW e-mails) - the early study
  (1 Oct) found the first flagged price was the biggest before the off 78% of the time.
- TEST on 39 days of live picks; re-checked at the end of November.
