## 0. What this is and is not
- **No keyword volume or competition numbers exist to us.** Etsy search terms, Marketplace Insights and Search Visibility are seller-only and not in the API. Every tag choice is therefore a **HYPOTHESIS** built from (a) the words already in the product, (b) the 23 Nov logged-out UK search baseline (ZANS ranks for silks/bauble/ornament terms and for course-specific guide terms, not for generic guide/gift terms) and (c) the coverage matrix. Nothing is claimed as proven.
- Facts quoted in openers (page counts, 7.5 cm, 11oz, colour + print PDF) are copied from the live listing text/attributes. No new claims, no AI mention, no 'stocking filler', no 'gamble responsibly' wording, no tips/prediction promise.
- All tags checked by script: 13 per listing, each unique within the listing, each 20 characters or fewer, letters/numbers/spaces only. Titles checked 140 characters or fewer.

- 30 days: 9 orders, £103.40 (green bauble 4, silks mug 1). Lifetime views: green bauble 76, silks mug 64, Ascot Flat Beginner 38, silks bauble 31, Ascot Flat Pro 8, white silks mug 7, Ascot Flat Advanced 5, Christmas mug 3, Cheltenham Beginner 1.
- **17 of 21 guides have 0 views** (Ascot Jumps x3, Cheltenham Pro/Adv, Kempton x6, Sandown x3, Aintree x3) = a discovery problem. Guide conversion cannot be judged until they get views.
- Tag overlap now: `horse racing gift` is on all 26 listings, `racecourse guide pdf` on 21, `gift for racing fan` on 20 (all guides). Tag clusters with NO coverage: syndicate / racing club / trainer (0), personalised (2 of 26), occasions beyond Christmas (thin).
- Hypothesis H1: 21 guides sharing 3 identical tags gives Etsy little to tell them apart, and the course-specific tags that DO differ are what the 23 Nov audit shows ZANS ranking for. So: more course/event-specific tags per guide, fewer shared ones.
- Hypothesis H2: gift words belong on Beginner guides (the gift buyer) and on Pro (title already says Gift); Advanced is a form/betting-study buyer, so it gets study words.
